# Article Instructions

This contract governs blog posts and articles — implementation stories, lessons learned,
retrospectives, and deep dives — for internal or external readers who want a clear narrative
with practical insight. The shared procedure is [document-authoring.md](document-authoring.md).

## Structure

Use this section order when it improves clarity.

1. `# Title`
2. `## Hook` — short opening that frames the problem or opportunity and why it matters.
3. `## Context` — relevant background and constraints.
4. `## Main Story` — chronological or thematic subsections.
5. `## Key Takeaways` — concise, actionable lessons or recommendations.
6. `## Next Steps` — optional actions, follow-ups, or call to action.
7. `## References` — links to related docs, repos, or resources, when there are any.

## Writing Rules

- Lead with reader value before implementation detail.
- Keep one narrative tense throughout the article.
- Ground every claim in a concrete example, outcome, or link.
- Keep paragraphs to 2-4 sentences and the story flow coherent.
